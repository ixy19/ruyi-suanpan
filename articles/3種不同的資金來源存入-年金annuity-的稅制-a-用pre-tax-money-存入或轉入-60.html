📌 購買年金（Annuity）的 3 種資金來源與稅制對照

年金本身只是一個「資產容器」，未來怎麼扣稅，完全取決於你當初是用什麼錢買的：

A. Pre-Tax（稅前資金）｜稱為 Qualified Annuity

資金來源：舊的 401(k)、403(b)、Traditional IRA / SEP IRA Rollover，或申報當年的 IRA 抵稅額。

稅務規則：

增長延稅：滾存期間免繳稅（Tax-Deferred）。

領錢全額課稅：未來領取的每一分錢（本金+增值）皆算作普通所得（Ordinary Income）課稅。

有 RMD 限制：受 73/75 歲強制提領（RMD）規定約束。

B. 銀行稅後現金｜稱為 Non-Qualified Annuity

資金來源：稅後存款（After-Tax Money）。

稅務規則：

無上限：沒有年度存入金額上限（No Contribution Limit）。

增長延稅：滾存期間免繳稅。

只有「增值」要課稅：領錢時採用 LIFO（後進先出） 原則，先領出的獲利部分認列為普通所得課稅，本金部分免稅；若採年金化（Annuitization）領取，則依比例免稅（Exclusion Ratio）。

無 RMD 限制：不受 mandatory RMD 約束。

C. Roth（免稅資金）｜稱為 Roth Annuity

資金來源：Roth 401(k)、Roth IRA Rollover，或申報當年的 Roth IRA Contribution。

稅務規則：

增長及提領皆免稅：符合條件提領（通常需滿 59.5 歲且帳戶滿 5 年），領出的本金與獲利100% 完全免稅。

無 RMD 限制：個人 Roth 年金無需強制提領。

3 Funding Sources & Tax Rules for Buying an Annuity

An annuity is essentially just an "asset bucket"—how it’s taxed down the road depends entirely on the type of money you use to fund it.

A. Pre-Tax Funding | Qualified Annuity

Funding Sources: Rollovers from an old 401(k), 403(b), Traditional IRA, or SEP IRA, as well as current-year tax-deductible IRA contributions.

Tax Rules:

Tax-Deferred Growth: Investments grow tax-free during the accumulation phase.

Fully Taxable Withdrawals: Every dollar withdrawn (principal + earnings) is taxed as ordinary income.

Subject to RMDs: Must follow Required Minimum Distribution rules starting at age 73/75.

B. After-Tax Cash | Non-Qualified Annuity

Funding Sources: Taxed savings / after-tax cash.

Tax Rules:

No Contribution Limits: You can invest as much as you want annually.

Tax-Deferred Growth: No tax owed while money stays in the account to grow.

Taxes Apply to Earnings Only: Withdrawals follow the LIFO (Last-In, First-Out) rule—earnings come out first and are taxed as ordinary income, while principal returns tax-free. If you opt for annuitization, distributions are tax-exempt based on the Exclusion Ratio.

No RMDs: Not subject to mandatory distribution requirements.

C. Roth (Tax-Free) Funding | Roth Annuity

Funding Sources: Roth 401(k) / Roth IRA rollovers, or current-year Roth IRA contributions.

Tax Rules:

100% Tax-Free Growth & Distributions: Qualified withdrawals (generally age 59½+ and an account age of at least 5 years) of both principal and gains are completely tax-free.

No RMDs: Personal Roth annuities do not require mandatory distributions.
