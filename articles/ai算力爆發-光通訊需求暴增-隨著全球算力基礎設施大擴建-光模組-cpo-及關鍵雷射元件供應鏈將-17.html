AI算力爆發，光通訊需求暴增

隨著全球算力基礎設施大擴建，光模組、CPO 及關鍵雷射元件供應鏈將是未來 2~3 年 AI 產業的核心主線

北美光通訊大廠 AAOI 最新財報釋放超強需求訊號。

 1. 雷射產能將擴大 10 倍以上：為因應 800G、1.6T 及未來 CPO 需求，管理層表示雷射產能增幅將遠超 10 倍，但可能依然不夠用。

 2. 高階光模組產能倍增：800G/1.6T 月產能預計從 Q1 底約 10 萬顆，今年底衝上 65 萬顆，2027 年達 93 萬顆/月。

3. 營收與訂單雙創高峰：Q2 營收年增 86%（資料中心年增 140%）；1.6T 已獲超 2 億美元訂單，Q3 底開始出貨。

「AI 爆發帶動了光通訊產業的大衝天」。

用一個生活化的比喻來看：

什麼是光通訊？ 如果把 AI 資料中心比喻成一座超大型工廠，裡面有幾萬台超級電腦在同時運算。電腦跟電腦之間傳輸資料，就像工廠裡的「運貨卡車」。以前用傳統銅線傳輸，速度太慢又會發熱；現在改用「光纖（光通訊）」，就像把卡車換成超音速高鐵。

需求超強： AI 算力太猛，資料傳輸量大暴增，所以各家大廠急著把傳輸速度升級（從 400G 升到 800G、甚至 1.6T）。

核心零件大缺貨（雷射晶片）： 要發出光信號就需要「雷射晶片」。這家廠商（AAOI）說要把產能擴大 10 倍以上，結果居然還說「可能還是不夠用」，代表市場需求旺到不行。

工廠瘋狂加班擴產（產能飆升）： 為了交貨，他們的產能從年初的每個月 10 萬顆，到年底要衝到 65 萬顆（成長超過 6 倍），訂單已經接到手軟。

賺到口袋滿滿（業績大爆發）： 營收比去年同期暴增 86%，單單最新一代（1.6T）產品就拿到了超過 2 億美元（約台幣 60 幾億）的訂單，而且這個季末就要開始出貨賺錢了。

一句話總結： AI 跑得太快，傳輸資料的「光學高鐵」不夠用，這家做光學零件的公司訂單接到滿出來，正準備大擴廠狠狠賺一筆。

"The AI boom is driving a massive surge in the optical communications industry."

Let's look at this through an everyday analogy:

What is Optical Communication?

If you think of an AI data center as a massive factory where tens of thousands of supercomputers are operating at the same time, transmitting data between these computers is like sending delivery trucks back and forth inside the factory. In the past, traditional copper wires were used, but they were too slow and overheated easily. Now, switching to fiber optics (optical communication) is like replacing those trucks with supersonic bullet trains.

Surging Demand: AI computing power is growing so fast that data transmission volume has exploded. As a result, major tech companies are rushing to upgrade their transmission speeds (from 400G to 800G, and even 1.6T).

Shortage of Core Components (Laser Chips): Emitting light signals requires "laser chips." This manufacturer (AAOI) plans to expand its production capacity by more than 10x, yet they stated it "might still not be enough," showing just how insanely high market demand is.

Ramping Up Production (Capacity Surge): To keep up with deliveries, their monthly capacity is ramping up from around 100,000 units at the start of the year to 650,000 units by year-end (more than a 6x increase). They are flooded with orders.

Record Revenue (Financial Explosion): Revenue jumped 86% year-over-year. For their latest-generation (1.6T) products alone, they secured over $200 million in orders (over NT$6 billion), with shipments set to start by the end of this quarter.

Bottom Line:

AI is moving too fast, and the data transmission "supersonic bullet train" can't keep up. This optical components manufacturer is overwhelmed with orders and expanding capacity rapidly to capitalize on the boom.
