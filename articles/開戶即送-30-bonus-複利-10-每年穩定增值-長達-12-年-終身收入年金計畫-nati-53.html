📌 核心機制與兩大關鍵釋疑：

「10% 複利」怎麼滾？（滾大基數，非提現存款）

首日加入 30% Bonus，且未領取前每年享 10% 增長率來「滾大你的退休金計算基數（Benefit Base）」。

關鍵釋疑：此基數專用於計算未來每年能領多少養老金，非一次性可提領的現金。這樣設計能幫你把未來的終身領取額度鎖定在最高點。

「最早 50 歲」就能開始領？（越早領額度小，越晚領領得多）

最早 50 歲即可啟動年金給付，一旦開啟，保證發放一輩子。

關鍵釋疑：50 歲就領，因基數滾存時間短且預期領取年限長，每年領取的金額較小；若放至 67 歲，基數滾得巨大，每年能領到的保證養老金會高出許多。

[Product Analysis] Nationwide High Point 365 Select (with Bonus Provision)

This Fixed Indexed Annuity (FIA) is designed specifically for pre-retirement planning. Its core purpose is to create guaranteed, lifelong cash flow that is unaffected by market volatility.

📌 Core Mechanism & 2 Key Clarifications:

How does the "10% Compound Interest" work? (Grows the base value; not a cash account balance)

Includes a 30% Bonus added on day one, plus a 10% growth rate per year prior to income withdrawals to grow your Benefit Base.

Key Clarification: This base is exclusively used to calculate future annual retirement payout amounts; it is not a lump-sum cash amount available for withdrawal. This design locks in your future lifelong payout limit at its highest point.

Can income start as early as age 50? (Earlier start = smaller payments; later start = larger payments)

Annuity payouts can be triggered as early as age 50. Once activated, payments are guaranteed for life.

Key Clarification: Taking payouts at age 50 results in smaller annual payments due to a shorter growth period and longer life expectancy for withdrawals. If deferred to age 67, the base grows substantially larger, resulting in significantly higher guaranteed annual pension income.

💡 Example Calculation (Based on a 55-year-old contributing $300,000 USD):

Purchase at Age 55: The Benefit Base immediately increases to $390,000.

Compounding to Age 67: The base grows to $1,223,987.

Payout Activation at Age 67: Guaranteed annual withdrawal of $57,772 (approx. $4,814/month), paid for as long as you live.

⚖️ Objective Assessment & Positioning:

Target Audience: Conservative investors seeking 100% risk mitigation and looking to lock in guaranteed retirement income ("Like a pension").

Important Considerations: For those seeking short-term capital growth or flexible cash liquidity, this product should be paired with other asset allocation tools.
