529 只能拿來付大學學費嗎？如果錢沒用完怎麼辦？🤔

很多人一聽到 529，就會想：

「如果小孩拿到全額獎學金，或根本不想上大學，那我存的錢不就卡住了？」

其實不用太擔心！😆

529 的彈性比你想像中大很多：

🎓 不只大學學費
符合規定的私立 K-12、職業教育、部分生活費、電腦設備，甚至部分學生貸款，都可能使用 529 支付。

👨‍👩‍👧 小孩不用，可以換受益人
如果老大用不完，可以在符合規定下轉給弟妹、其他家人，甚至留給下一代使用。

🏆 拿到獎學金也有解
符合條件時，可以提領相當於獎學金金額的部分，並免除通常的 10% 額外罰款。

💰 甚至可以接力到 Roth IRA
符合 SECURE 2.0 規定的 529 帳戶，在滿足條件下，部分未使用資金可以轉到受益人的 Roth IRA，終身上限 $35,000。

🎁 而且不只是爸媽能存
阿公阿嬤、親朋好友都可以幫忙存；有些公司甚至提供 529 福利。手上有一大筆資金的人，也可以考慮 529 Superfunding，一次投入最多 5 年的贈與稅年度排除額。

所以，529 並不是：

❌「小孩沒上大學，錢就浪費了」

反而更像是：

「教育優先，但錢沒用完還有其他合法的出口。」

當然，529 也不是想領就隨時領的普通投資帳戶。非合格提領通常會涉及收益所得稅和 10% 額外罰款，所以怎麼存、存多少，還是要看每個家庭的情況。

Can You Only Use a 529 Plan for College? What Happens If You Don’t Use All the Money? 🤔

A lot of people hear “529 Plan” and think:

“What if my child gets a full scholarship—or doesn’t go to college at all? Is my money stuck there?”

Actually, not at all! 😆

A 529 Plan is more flexible than you might think:

🎓 It’s not just for college tuition.
Depending on the rules, 529 funds may be used for eligible K-12 private school expenses, vocational education, certain living expenses, computers, and even some student loan payments.

👨‍👩‍👧 You can change the beneficiary.
If one child doesn’t need all the money, you may be able to transfer the account to a sibling or another eligible family member—or even save it for the next generation.

🏆 What if your child gets a scholarship?
Under certain circumstances, you can withdraw an amount equal to the scholarship without the usual 10% additional penalty.

💰 You may even be able to roll some of it into a Roth IRA.
Under SECURE 2.0 rules, eligible unused 529 funds can potentially be rolled into the beneficiary’s Roth IRA, subject to certain requirements and a $35,000 lifetime limit.

🎁 And it’s not just parents who can contribute!
Grandparents, relatives, and friends can contribute too. Some employers even offer 529 benefits. And if you have a large amount of money to contribute, you may also consider 529 “superfunding” to take advantage of multiple years of gift-tax exclusions.

So, a 529 Plan is NOT:

❌ “If my child doesn’t go to college, the money is wasted.”

It’s more like:

“Education comes first—but if the money isn’t fully used, there are still other options.”

Of course, a 529 isn’t a regular investment account where you can withdraw money anytime without consequences. Non-qualified withdrawals may be subject to income tax and a 10% additional penalty on earnings.
