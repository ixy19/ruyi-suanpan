美國的州遺產稅&及繼承稅 
美國共有 12 個州以及華盛頓特區 徵收州層級的「遺產稅」(Estate Tax)。這些州的免稅額與聯邦遺產稅是脫鉤的，且大多遠低於聯邦門檻，代表即使資產未達聯邦課稅標準，仍可能面臨州級稅負。
州免遺產稅額度，例如：
Oregon 僅100萬美金
Massachusetts 僅200萬美金
Washington 僅300萬
Illinois 僅400萬
Maryland 僅500萬

繼承稅 (Inheritance Tax)
上述的「遺產稅」是針對過世者的遺產總額課徵；而「繼承稅」則是針對受益人（拿錢的人）課徵，稅率高低取決於繼承人與死者的親屬關係。通常配偶與直系血親可完全豁免繼承稅，關係越遠或無血緣關係者，稅率越高。目前全美有 5 個州徵收州繼承稅，例如NJ, PA, MD state

US State Estate Tax & Inheritance Tax

12 US states and Washington, D.C. collect state-level Estate Tax. These state exemptions are separate from the federal estate tax and are usually much lower than the federal threshold. This means you may owe state taxes even if your estate isn't taxed at the federal level.

Examples of state estate tax exemptions:

Oregon: $1 million

Massachusetts: $2 million

Washington: $3 million

Illinois: $4 million

Maryland: $5 million

Inheritance Tax

While an "Estate Tax" taxes the total value of the deceased person's estate, an "Inheritance Tax" taxes the beneficiaries (the people receiving the money). The tax rate depends on the beneficiary's relationship to the deceased. Usually, spouses and direct family members are fully exempt. Non-relatives or distant relatives pay higher rates. Currently, 5 states collect inheritance tax, including NJ, PA, and MD.
