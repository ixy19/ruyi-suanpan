3 分鐘看懂：為什麼美國房貸變這麼貴？

觀念先修：什麼是「債券殖利率」？

想像你借錢給美國政府（買美國國債），政府開出一張借據：

• 借據內容：「你給我 100 塊，我每年給 5 塊 利息。」（報酬率/殖利率為 5%）

• 市場拋售： 後來大家不想要這張借據，打折拋售到只剩 90 塊。

• 殖利率飆升： 新買家只花 90 塊 同樣能拿到 5 塊 利息，報酬率（殖利率）就暴升到 5.5% 以上。

核心邏輯：債券價格越跌，殖利率（報酬率）就越高！

新聞白話解讀：美債與房貸現況

美債利息飆到 5.146% 創下近 18 年新高！

背後主要有三大原因逼得政府必須開出高利息才有人願意借錢：

1. 政府狂賣債券： 財政赤字過大，市場上的借據太多（供過於求）。

2. 科技巨頭搶資金： 微軟、Google 等 AI 大廠也跑出來高薪/高息搶著借錢。

3. 通膨降不下來： 國際局勢動盪導致物價居高不下，大家怕錢變薄，要求更高的回報才肯出借。

最終影響： 美國政府借錢的利息（美債殖利率）是全市場的基準。當政府借錢都要付 5% 以上利息時，銀行放貸給民眾買房自然更貴，導致房貸利率跟著飆升到 6.55%，貴到民眾根本不敢辦理轉貸或再融資了。

3-Minute Guide: Why Have U.S. Mortgage Rates Gotten So Expensive?

Prerequisite Concept: What is "Bond Yield"?

Imagine you lend money to the U.S. government (by buying a U.S. Treasury bond), and the government issues you an IOU:

The Original IOU: "You give me $100, and I'll pay you $5 in interest every year." (Yield/Return = 5%)

Market Sell-off: Later, people start dumping these IOUs, driving the discounted price down to $90.

Yields Surge: A new buyer now only spends $90 to get the same $5 interest, driving their rate of return (yield) up to over 5.5%.

Core Logic: As bond prices drop, bond yields go UP!

News Breakdown: U.S. Treasuries & The Current Mortgage Market

U.S. Treasury yields have surged to 5.146%, hitting a nearly 18-year high!

There are three major reasons forcing the government to offer higher interest rates just to get people to lend them money:

Government Flood of Bonds: Massive fiscal deficits mean too many IOUs are hitting the market (oversupply).

Tech Giants Competing for Capital: AI powerhouses like Microsoft and Google are also issuing high-yield debt to borrow big money.

Sticky Inflation: Global geopolitical turmoil is keeping prices high, making investors demand higher returns to combat inflation.

The Bottom Line Impact

The rate at which the U.S. government borrows money (U.S. Treasury yield) serves as the benchmark for the entire market.

When even the government has to pay over 5% interest to borrow, banks naturally charge everyday homebuyers significantly more. This has pushed mortgage rates up to 6.55%—making it so expensive that homeowners are avoiding refinancing altogether.
