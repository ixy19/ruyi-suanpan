什麼是「川普帳戶」（530A 帳戶）？一文看懂！

簡單來說，這是美國政府給新生兒的「國家級長期定存+投資包」，幫小孩從出生就開始存第一桶金。

核心機制：它是怎麼運作的？

政府免費送 $1,000 美金：

只要寶寶在 2025 到 2028 年間 出生、是美國公民且有 SSN（社安號），財政部直接在帳戶裡送你 $1,000 種子基金，不拿白不拿！

每年最多存 $5,000：

爸媽、阿公阿嬤、親友都可以存。最佛心的是公司幫忙存：如果雇主幫你小孩的帳戶貼錢，每年最多 $2,500 不算在你的個人所得稅裡，公司還能拿去抵稅，雙贏！

強迫投資 S&P 500，18 年不准動：

錢存進去後，會直接投進低手續費的全美大盤股票 ETF（像 S&P 500）。在小孩滿 18 歲前完全鎖死、不能提領，讓時間幫你滾出滾雪球般的複利效果。

18 歲解鎖變 IRA：

滿 18 歲後帳戶會移交給小孩自己管，轉成 Traditional IRA。這筆錢可以用來付大學學費、考證照、買第一間房或創業。如果亂花挪作他用，除了要繳稅，還會被扣額外的罰款！

快速衡量：優缺點一次看

值得辦的優點：

白拿 $1,000： 政府給的免費紅包，不領真的太可惜。

雇主福利大方： 公司如果有給這項 Perks，每年 $2,500 免稅額簡直是免費送錢。

強迫複利滾錢： 標的鎖定大盤且手續費極低（≤0.1%），加上 18 年鎖死機制，爸媽不用擔心自己忍不住提早把錢花掉。

需要注意的缺點：

彈性超低： 18 歲前完全提不出來，急用錢時派不上用場。

省稅不如 529： 如果你這筆錢100% 只想拿來付大學學費，529 計劃的獲利是「完全免稅」；但川普帳戶拿來付學費雖然免罰款，獲利部分還是要算所得繳稅。

18 歲控制權變小孩的： 滿 18 歲錢就歸小孩管了，如果孩子當時理財觀念還不成熟，一口氣亂領可能會被扣高額稅金與罰款。

最佳口袋策略

基本動作： 符合條件的新生兒，政府那 $1,000 絕對要領！

進階操作： 如果你的公司有提供對等資助（Match），一定要存滿公司幫忙出的額度，不拿白不上。

配置建議： 如果你的主要目標是準備大學學費，請優先把錢放 529 計畫；「川普帳戶」則很適合當作幫孩子準備成年後創業、買房或超長期理財的額外加碼工具！

What is a "Trump Account" (Section 530A)? Everything You Need to Know!Simply put, it’s a government-backed long-term investment package for newborns, designed to give children a head start on their financial future right from birth. Core Mechanics: How Does It Work?$1,000 Free Seed Money from the Government:Any child born between 2025 and 2028 who is a U.S. citizen with a valid Social Security Number (SSN) will receive a one-time $1,000 initial grant directly from the U.S. Treasury. It's essentially free money!Up to $5,000 Annual Contribution Cap:Parents, relatives, friends, or even employers can contribute. The biggest highlight is employer assistance: if an employer contributes to your child's account, up to $2,500 per year is tax-free for the employee (not included in W-2 taxable income) and fully tax-deductible for the employer as a payroll expense.Mandatory S&P 500 Indexing & 18-Year Lockup:Funds are automatically invested in low-cost, broad-market index ETFs (like S&P 500 or Total Stock Market ETFs). No withdrawals are allowed before the child turns 18, forcing full exposure to long-term compound growth.Unlocks at Age 18 into a Traditional IRA:When the child turns 18, account management transfers directly to them as a Traditional IRA. The funds can be used for college tuition, professional certifications, first-time home purchases, or starting a business. Unauthorized early withdrawals will incur income tax plus extra penalties. Quick Assessment: Pros & Cons Pros (Why It’s Great):Free $1,000 Grant: A government-funded kickstart you shouldn't miss.Great Employer Benefit: If your company offers this perk, the $2,500 tax-free benefit is essentially free compensation.Forced Compounding: Capped expense ratios ($\le 0.1\%$) combined with an 18-year lockup ensure parents won't accidentally dip into the savings prematurely. Cons & Considerations:Zero Liquidity: Funds are completely locked until age 18—no emergency access whatsoever.Inferior Tax Perks for Education vs. 529: If your goal is 100% college tuition, 529 earnings are completely tax-free upon withdrawal. Trump Account withdrawals for tuition avoid early penalties, but earnings are still taxed as ordinary income.Control Shifts at Age 18: Full control transfers to the child at 18. If they lack financial maturity, cashing out recklessly could trigger heavy taxes and penalties. Best Practical StrategyBaseline Move: If you have an eligible newborn, claim the government’s $1,000 grant without hesitation.Next Level: If your employer offers a contribution match, contribute enough to max out the tax-free $2,500 employer benefit.Asset Allocation: If your primary goal is saving for college tuition, prioritize a 529 Plan. Use the "Trump Account" as a complementary tool for ultra-long-term wealth building, home buying, or entrepreneurship later in life!
